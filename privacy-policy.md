# WalkFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

WalkFlow is a focus timer and active recovery app. It helps you complete a focus session, take a short walking break, and unlock the next session.

## Data WalkFlow Uses

WalkFlow uses step and motion data only while you are using recovery missions in the app. This data is used to show your recovery step progress and unlock the next focus session.

WalkFlow may also store app settings, session history, daily summary values, XP, streaks, badges, timer preferences, and onboarding status locally on your device.

## Local-Only Storage

WalkFlow does not require an account and does not send your focus sessions, step counts, settings, or progress history to a server.

Your WalkFlow data is stored locally on your device. If you reset local data in the app or uninstall the app, this local data may be removed.

## Permissions

WalkFlow may request these permissions:

- Activity Recognition or motion access: used to count steps during active recovery missions.
- Notifications: used only if you enable optional reminders or summary notifications.
- Haptics or vibration: used for optional touch feedback.

You can deny optional permissions. Some features, such as live step tracking or notifications, may not work without the related permission.

## Data Sharing

WalkFlow does not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

WalkFlow does not include ads, account tracking, or third-party analytics in this version.

## Children

WalkFlow is intended for general productivity and wellness use. It is not directed at children.
